# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), deployed to Vercel or Netlify. Mobile-first web; no native app.

## Users

Patients, caregivers, and people actively seeking healthcare services. They arrive while something is wrong or someone they care for needs care, often on a phone, often under time pressure. Secondary audience: caregivers managing records and appointments on someone else's behalf.

## Product Purpose

Medicare is a healthcare navigation and assistance platform. It lets people discover nearby hospitals, find doctors and book appointments, reach emergency healthcare resources, store medical records, and get guidance from iCare AI (symptom guidance and plain-language explanations of medical reports). Success means a person under stress reaches the right care with fewer steps and less doubt.

## Positioning

One trusted place for the whole navigation job — find care, book it, keep records, and understand results — rather than a directory, a booking widget, or a symptom checker on its own. iCare AI's plain-language report explanation is the differentiating capability.

## Operating Context

Used on mobile, in the moment: searching for a nearby hospital, comparing doctors, booking a slot, checking what to do in an emergency, uploading or looking up a record, pasting or photographing a report for explanation. Caregivers use it on behalf of another person. Emergency and urgent situations are a first-class path, not an afterthought.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hospital discovery by proximity, doctor search, appointment booking.
- Emergency healthcare resources.
- Medical record storage.
- iCare AI: symptom guidance and medical report explanation.
- Healthcare compliance matters: privacy, consent, and clinical-safety wording are constraints on all future work.
- Undecided: whether hospital/doctor/provider data is real or realistic sample data; whether record storage and iCare AI are backed by real services or demonstrations; authentication model; HIPAA posture and any regulatory claims.
- iCare AI output must never read as diagnosis or a substitute for professional care.

## Brand Commitments

Product name is Medicare; the AI assistant is named iCare AI. Voice must be calm, plain-language, and trustworthy — never alarmist, never salesy.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No code, assets, provider data, logos, testimonials, or compliance certifications exist yet. Future work must not fabricate hospital networks, doctor credentials, patient testimonials, certifications, or clinical claims.

## Product Principles

1. Calm under stress — every screen assumes the user is worried and in a hurry.
2. Plain language over clinical jargon, everywhere, including AI output.
3. Emergency paths are never buried behind a flow.
4. Trust is shown, not claimed — no invented data, credentials, or assurances.
5. Mobile-first and accessible by default, not as a later pass.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first use on small screens; accessibility is a stated product goal (simple, trustworthy, accessible experience). No specific standard (e.g. WCAG level) was confirmed — treat WCAG 2.1 AA as the working target and record a different level if the user sets one.
